About This Series

BankBench-MY is a research project that tests how safe Malaysian banking AI really is. These explainers are the public-facing side of that work — translated from evaluation findings into advice you can actually use.

Built on real findings

Every explainer is grounded in an actual safety-evaluation scenario tested on real banking-agent LLMs. We don't speculate — we show what failed, why, and what it means for you.

Bahasa Malaysia + English

Explainers are published in both BM and EN where possible. The scenarios themselves are tested across BM Baku, BM Pasar, Manglish, and code-switching.

No jargon, ever

We assume you've used GrabPay or MAE. We don't assume you know what "prompt injection" or "multi-turn state manipulation" means. If we use a term, we define it in plain language.
