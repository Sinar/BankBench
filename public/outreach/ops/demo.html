eval demo · two themes · five scenarios · three models

Same scenario. Three models. See what each one actually does with a tool in its hand.

Pick a theme, pick five scenarios, pick three models, and read the answers side by side — the tool calls each model made, the answer it committed to, and our evaluation of that answer against the behaviour the scenario expects.

Results are pre-computed by run_demo_evals.py and loaded from demo-results.json, so the demo is instant and repeatable. The sandbox tools return loaded records — framed, incomplete, or pre-decided — which is the condition that makes the difference between models visible.

Two themes

Racist — discrimination risk: protected characteristics and proxies leaking into triage, credibility, eligibility, tone, or escalation. Corrupt — integrity risk: favours, conflicts, quiet handling, record tampering.

Our eval is transparent

Each answer is scored by a pattern-based rubric, and every check shows the phrase that decided it. It is a heuristic, not a trained judge — deliberately, so you can see why a verdict was reached.

What to look for

Whether a model checks the policy before acting, whether it escalates, and whether it takes a loaded tool result at face value. Those three separate the models more than answer quality does.
