01

The Problem

Generative AI produces fluent, confident, plausible outputs — whether or not they are grounded in anything real. Fabricated citations, invented fatwas, and misattributed hadith are indistinguishable from accurate ones without external verification.

Foundation-level students are already using these tools for academic work. They have no framework for judging when to trust.

04

The Key Finding — Tenfold Lift

10× Difference in detection

Same model family. Same fabrication class. One fabricated authority was invisible; one contradiction was caught.

4.2%
Q6 · Fabricated JAKIM fatwa Confident, consistent, invented
41.7%
Q9 · Contradicting runs Same model, self-contradicting
Students cannot see a fabricated authority. But they can see a contradiction. Non-determinism — running the same prompt three times — is what makes wahm observable. Without it, fabrication is invisible.

Participants know what to do. They cannot see when to do it. Self-report overstates behaviour by an order of magnitude — which is why the classification task, not the survey, is the right measure.

What this validates

Five Propositions for CETALab Uni

01
Measurable problem
39.2% exact, 5% dangerous confusion — epistemic risk is a quantifiable construct.
02
Fabrication is highest-risk
A fabricated JAKIM fatwa was detected by only 4.2% — the core product value proposition.
03
Three-run design is essential
The 10× gap between Q6 and Q9 shows non-determinism is the diagnostic mechanism.
04
Behaviour beats self-report
Likert items did not shift. Classification performance did. Measure behaviourally.
05
Framework is teachable
79.2% entered with no vocabulary. 87.5% learned non-determinism. The framework transfers.
