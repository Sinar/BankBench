The use of divisive rhetoric by some politicians has insidious effects that cannot be
underestimated. Countries which have higher incidences of hateful political rhetoric tend
to have a higher level of violence through domestic violence—with an average of 107.9
domestic terrorist attacks between 2000-2017.” Many governments acknowledge the
aggravating factor when politicians engage in hate speech, thus certain legal systems have
increased punishments for offenders in this category. At present, Malaysian law does not
differentiate offenders based on their level of influence, which could contribute to the
indifference shown by certain politicians who continue to participate in the ‘politics of
hate.’
