7.2 Strengthening National & Legal Frameworks
There is a great need to address weaknesses in the current legal framework to manage hate
speech. This is because some of the laws today are rather vague and general that they may
result in unjust restrictions on free speech. This is why a specific law on hate speech is
important—not to be more restrictive, but to provide a comprehensive, clear legal guideline
that could deter arbitrary persecution.

Contrary to popular belief, having a specific law to regulate hate speech is not meant
to be more restrictive of speech, because it is dangerous if all types of offensive speech are
made punishable under the law. Rather, it should aim to provide a higher standard as to
what falls under hate speech to avoid abuse of process. The lawmaking process must also
