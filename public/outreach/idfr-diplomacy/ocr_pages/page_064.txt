4.0 EXISTING LEGAL FRAMEWORK TO ADDRESS HATE SPEECH
4.2 Issues with Legal Definitions
There are several problems regarding the definitions of offences that are wide enough to
cover issues related to hate speech. However, many of the key terms that appear in these
laws are either defined ambiguously, and hate speech is not defined at all. The issues that
persist related to definitions of terms related to hate speech may result in many instances
of speech that can be cast under the grey area of these laws—some of which may not
necessarily need legal intervention and restriction. The table below (Figure 20) highlights
some provisions in the Malaysian legal framework which could apply to hate speech:
