Virulent and hate-laden advocacy
can trigger the worst of crimes.
Suffice it to recall recent examples
of post-electoral violence spurred by
hatred along ethnic lines; incidents
involving extremist groups; abusive
and malicious portrayal, online or in
traditional media, of certain religions
and their followers. It is clear that
hatred has many faces and is present
in all parts of the world.

Annual Report of the United Nations High Commissioner
for Human Rights (2003)
