In sum, Islamophobia works
in both Western and Muslim
countries as a discriminatory
practice against what is perceived
as ‘civilizational backwardness’ that
legitimizes, if necessary, the use
of violence, as can be seen with
deadly military crackdowns on free
elections. This dynamic is intensified
and strengthened by the constant
humiliation of Muslims and Muslim
lifestyle in the media and social
media in Muslim countries.
