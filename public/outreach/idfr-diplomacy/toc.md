## Highest-leverage contributions (core expertise)

### Chapter 5: AI Safety — strongest fit
- **5.1 Risk Assessment** — apply structured AI risk taxonomy / likelihood-severity framing to diplomatic use cases.
  - Bengio, Y. et al. (2024). "Managing Extreme AI Risks amid Rapid Progress." *Science*, 384(6698), 842–845. https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adn0117
  - NIST (2023). *AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)*. https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **5.2 Categorisation of Risks** — extend the Low-Impact vs High-Stakes framing with a graduated safety tier model (e.g., admin support → analysis → negotiation/decision support).
  - EU AI Act (2024) — risk-tier classification (unacceptable/high/limited/minimal risk). Official text: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/the-act/
  - Reuel, A., Bucknall, B. et al. (2024). "Open Problems in Technical AI Governance." *TMLR*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.14981 *(already sourced in project Drive as "Chp 2.4")*
- **5.3 Evaluation** — contribute evaluation & benchmarking practices (capability evals, scenario testing) adapted for diplomatic workflows.
  - Shevlane, T. et al. (2023). "Model Evaluation for Extreme Risks." *arXiv:2305.15324*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15324
- **5.4 Red-Teaming** — strongest single contribution: adversarial testing, jailbreak/resilience probing, tabletop exercises for diplomatic AI misuse.
  - Ganguli, D. et al. / Anthropic (2022). "Red Teaming Language Models to Reduce Harms: Methods, Scaling Behaviors, and Lessons Learned." https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07858
  - Anthropic. "Claude System Card" (frontier model red-teaming disclosures). https://www.anthropic.com/claude-4-system-card
  - OpenAI. "GPT-4 System Card." https://cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4-system-card.pdf
- **5.5 Internal AI Guidelines and Safe Use Policies** — draft concrete safe-use policy templates for foreign ministries (model access tiers, human-in-the-loop mandates, prohibited uses).
  - NIST AI RMF 1.0 (as above) — structure for a ministry safe-use policy.
  - OWASP Gen AI Security Project — Top 10 for LLM Applications. https://genai.owasp.org/

  - UN Secretary-General's High-Level Panel on Digital Cooperation (2019). "The Age of Digital Interdependence." https://www.un.org/en/pdfs/DigitalCooperation-report-for%20web.pdf
