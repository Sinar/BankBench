1 · The symbiosis — why the four domains need each other

No single domain can produce assurance alone. Each holds a vantage the others cannot substitute for, and each is structurally blind in a way the others are not. The four feed one shared layer; the layer returns evidence all four can act on. That loop — not any single project — is the spine of the work.

What the layer is actually for

  • One demand, four dialects. Each domain asks the same question in different words: is this technology useful, safe, accountable and worth supporting?
  • Requirements recur; mandates do not. The same test framework cannot erase sector-specific obligations — so the method travels and the compliance claim stays home.
  • Evidence moves, data does not. Shared schemas and methods; separately controlled source data; selectively shared findings.
  • Scrutiny is load-bearing. CSOs and communities catch what bank workflow logs and training curricula structurally cannot see.

A bank sees interactions with its own services. A complaints organisation sees people who seek help. A community organisation may reach people who never use the service or never complain. None of these is a complete population sample — which is why triangulation matters more than assembling a larger dataset.
